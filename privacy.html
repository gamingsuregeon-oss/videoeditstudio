Privacy Policy

VideoEdit Studio · Last updated: October 4, 2026

This policy explains what information the VideoEdit Studio app ("the app") handles. The app is provided by Timbakai ("we", "us").

Information we collect

We do not require an account, and we do not collect your name, email address, phone number or location. We do not run our own servers that receive your data.

Your videos and media

Videos, photos and audio that you choose to edit are processed on your device only. We do not upload them to us or to any server controlled by us. The app accesses only the files you select.

Advertising (Unity Ads)

The app shows rewarded video ads provided by Unity Ads, a service of Unity Technologies. Watching an ad is optional and is used to remove the watermark or unlock 2K and 4K export.

To serve and measure ads, Unity may collect and process certain information from your device, such as your IP address, device identifiers (including the advertising ID), device and app information, and how you interact with ads. We ask Unity to show contextual (non-personalized) ads, so ads are not based on your behavior across apps. This data is handled by Unity under its own privacy policy: unity.com/legal/privacy-policy.

You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android device settings (Settings → Privacy → Ads).

Children

The app is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Data security

Because your media stays on your device, it is protected by your device's own security. Ad-related data handled by Unity is protected under Unity's policies.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version is always on this page, with the date above.
